# Bee Movie (2007) 🐝

## About the Movie

**Bee Movie** is an animated comedy film released in 2007 and directed by Simon J. Smith and Steve Hickner.

The story follows Barry B. Benson, a bee who graduates from college and becomes disappointed with the idea of working the same job for the rest of his life. He eventually discovers that humans are stealing honey from bees and decides to take legal action against them.

## Main Characters

- **Barry B. Benson** – The main character, a bee who questions the rules of bee society.
- **Vanessa Bloome** – A human florist who becomes Barry's friend.
- **Adam Flayman** – Barry's best friend.
- **Ken** – Vanessa's boyfriend.

## My Favorite Part

*My favorite part is when Barry decides to sue the entire human race for stealing honey. It is funny and unexpected because a bee actually takes humans to court.*
